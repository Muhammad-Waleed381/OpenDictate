# OpenDictate

A private, offline dictation app for the desktop. People press a hotkey or say a wake phrase, speak in any app, and their words are typed there. Speech recognition runs entirely on their own machine.

**Who uses it:** people who write a lot and care about privacy, including developers dictating to terminals and AI coding tools. It is opened briefly to check setup and tune settings, then mostly runs in the background behind a small floating dock.

**What the main window is for:** confirming dictation is ready, starting a recording, reviewing what was inserted, and managing models, the dictionary, snippets and settings.

**Principles:** nothing leaves the device, and the UI says so plainly. The app stays quiet until it is recording.
